About this project

VESPER is developed as part of the HEDREMO project. VESPER may assist with reading eligible messages, drafting responses, and sending authorized replies when a platform connection and its permissions permit those functions.

Some features are in development and may not be available to all users. Connections are subject to the relevant platform's permissions and policies.

Your choices

Access to connected accounts is granted through the applicable platform's authorization process. You can revoke access from the relevant platform's connected-app settings. For information about deleting data held by VESPER, see our data deletion page.
