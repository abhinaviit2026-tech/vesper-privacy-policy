Data Deletion Instructions

How to disconnect a service and request removal of information stored by VESPER.

Updated: 22 November 2026

1. Disconnect the app

To stop future account access, revoke VESPER's authorization from your relevant Meta or Google account settings, using the connected applications or business integrations area. You may also disable the connection in VESPER if your installation exposes a disconnect feature. Disconnecting does not automatically delete previously stored local data.

2. Request data deletion

  1. Send a request to abhinaviit2026@gmail.com.
  2. Use the subject line VESPER Data Deletion Request.
  3. Provide the platform (Instagram, WhatsApp Business, or Gmail), your account username or non-sensitive identifier, and which information you would like deleted.
  4. We may ask for reasonable verification that you control the relevant connected account before deleting its records.

Never include your password, API access token, or authentication code in the request.

3. What can be requested

Where held by the VESPER deployment, you may request deletion of stored account identifiers, OAuth credentials, message content, conversation history, task records and associated local agent context. Records owned by Meta, Google, or other platforms must be managed through those platforms' own data controls.

4. Processing and exceptions

The operator will review the request, identify matching records, and confirm the result through the supplied contact channel. Some information may be temporarily retained in operational backups or where necessary for security, dispute resolution or legal obligations. This page does not promise a specific processing deadline until an operational procedure is verified and published.

5. Questions

For questions about deletion or the handling of connected-account information, email abhinaviit2026@gmail.com. See the Privacy Policy for details about processing.
