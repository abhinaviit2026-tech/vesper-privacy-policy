Privacy Policy

How VESPER AI handles information when you choose to connect messaging and email services.

Effective date: 22 November 2026 · Project: VESPER AI (HEDREMO)

1. Who we are

VESPER AI is an AI assistant project operated under the HEDREMO project name. HEDREMO is a project name and is not presented here as a registered corporation. For questions about this policy, contact: abhinaviit2026@gmail.com.

2. Scope

This policy concerns VESPER's optional integrations with Instagram Professional accounts, WhatsApp Business and Gmail, and related communication-assistant functions. Capabilities vary by account, platform permissions and development status. Merely visiting this informational website does not connect your social accounts.

3. Information we may process

  • Account connection data: platform account identifiers, usernames, authorized business/Page identifiers, permissions and access credentials obtained through platform authorization.
  • Communication data: messages, email content, sender and recipient identifiers, thread identifiers, timestamps, and attachments when necessary to perform an authorized task.
  • Agent task data: user instructions, drafts, execution results, conversation context, task state, and operational audit/error records.
  • Technical information: webhook events and identifiers needed to verify, process and deduplicate platform events.

We do not require your Instagram, Facebook or Google account password to authorize their official API connections.

4. Why we use information

Data is used to provide features you authorize, such as notifying you of incoming messages, displaying or summarizing eligible conversations, preparing replies, sending approved communications, and keeping the context of ongoing assigned tasks. We may also process technical logs to troubleshoot errors, protect connections and prevent duplicate actions.

5. AI processing and service providers

When an AI-assisted task is enabled, relevant portions of messages or instructions may be sent to the configured AI model provider (for example, Google's Gemini API) to generate a response or summary. Platform operators such as Meta and Google process data under their own terms and privacy policies. The precise provider and data-sharing configuration may vary by deployment. Do not connect accounts until you have reviewed and accepted the applicable configuration.

6. Sharing and sales

VESPER is designed to share information with connected platforms and processing services only when necessary to perform an authorized function. This project does not intend to sell message contents or account identifiers to advertisers or data brokers. Any future material change to these practices should be reflected in this policy before deployment.

7. Storage, retention and security

Depending on deployment, VESPER may store authorization credentials, conversation records and agent task state locally or on the configured hosting environment. Access should be limited to the operator and authorized software components. No online transmission or storage system is completely secure.

Retention: records may remain until they are deleted by the operator or in response to a verified deletion request, subject to technical backups and applicable obligations. A fixed automated deletion period is not currently represented here; the implementation should be confirmed before launching to third-party users.

8. Your controls and rights

You can stop an agent task, revoke platform access through Meta or Google account settings, and request removal of information stored by VESPER. Revoking access stops future authorized API activity but may not automatically erase data that was previously stored. See the Data Deletion Instructions.

9. Permissions and messaging limits

VESPER accesses only the information made available through the connected account's permissions and eligible APIs. Messaging capabilities may be limited by platform policies, account roles, app review and messaging windows. VESPER does not claim unrestricted access to personal or professional inboxes.

10. Children and sensitive information

VESPER's communication automation is not designed for children. Users should avoid sending passwords, payment-card details or other sensitive secrets through AI-managed conversations. If such content is received unexpectedly, request deletion and revoke access as appropriate.

11. Data requests and responses

To request access to or deletion of data held by a VESPER installation operated by this project, email abhinaviit2026@gmail.com with the subject “VESPER Privacy Request.” Include the platform and enough non-sensitive information to identify the relevant account. We may ask you to verify account ownership before disclosing or deleting data. If an installation is self-hosted by someone else, its operator may separately control the data stored there.

12. Updates

We may revise this policy as VESPER's functionality changes. The effective date above will be updated when material changes are published.

13. Contact

For access, deletion or privacy questions, email abhinaviit2026@gmail.com and identify the connected platform or account sufficiently for the operator to locate the relevant data. Do not include account passwords or API tokens.
